# Garou: Mark of the Wolves — Training Music Patch

This patch restores background music in **Training Mode**\.

## Requirements

- A clean, matching **Garou: Mark of the Wolves P4 ROM**
- `garou_training_music_patch.bps`
- A BPS patching tool, such as **Floating IPS \(Flips\)**

## Applying the patch

1. Back up your original P4 ROM\.
2. Open your BPS patching tool and choose **Apply Patch**\.
3. Select `garou_training_music_patch.bps`\.
4. Select the clean Garou P4 ROM as the source\.
5. Save the patched P4 ROM\.

Do not apply the patch to an already patched ROM\.

## What it fixes

The patch restores Training Mode music when:

- Entering Training Mode\.
- Changing the stage\.
- Changing characters and returning to the training fight\.
- Returning characters to the center of the stage\.

The music selection follows the current stage\.

## Notes

The patch only modifies the **P4 ROM**\. Keep the rest of the Garou ROM set unchanged\.

Keep a backup of the original ROM and verify that the patched ROM has the expected size for your setup\.
